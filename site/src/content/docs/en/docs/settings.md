Open **Settings** from the palette footer. It is a separate window for configuring Clackly while retaining your Resolve workspace.

## General settings

In **General Settings**, check the version, switch between **English / 简体中文**, or [check for updates](/Clackly/en/docs/updates/). Language changes apply immediately to the palette and open Settings.

## Features and dependencies

1. Choose a feature in the sidebar and check its status and required settings.
2. Complete the configuration, such as the After Effects path for AE export.
3. Click **Save changes** and check readiness again.

**Discard changes** restores the last saved values. Leaving unsaved configuration prompts for confirmation. **Enabled** does not mean ready: resolve any missing dependencies shown in the status.
